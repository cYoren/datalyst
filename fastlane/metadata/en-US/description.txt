Datalyst is a symptom and habit tracker. Log how you feel and what you do, then see how they line up.

Log a migraine, a night of sleep, a coffee, a walk, or a mood score. After a couple of weeks, Datalyst shows which habits move with your symptoms. The numbers are real correlations, with a plain note that a pattern is not proof of cause.

What you can do
• Log symptoms and habits in a daily check-in
• Look back at any day in your history
• See which habits line up with how you feel
• Turn a hunch into a personal experiment

Datalyst is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or give medical advice.
